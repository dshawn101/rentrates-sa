A QUICK CONTINUITY GUIDE

What goes in a series bible before Book 2?

A series bible is a reference for decisions your next book needs to respect. Start with details that would confuse readers if they changed. Add a book and chapter reference when you know where a fact was established.

Character facts
Names, relationships and details that should stay consistent.
Changes by book
What changed, when it happened, and what’s true at the start of the next book.
World rules
Rules for places, technology or magic, including any established exceptions.
Open threads
Questions you’ve left with readers and where you intend to resolve them.

Example: The Lantern Coast

Mara never learned to swim. In Book 1, chapter 12, she gave the lighthouse key to Ivo. The lantern works only below the tide line. The sender of the unmarked map is still unknown when Book 2 opens.

The notes generator organises what you enter. The separate Book #2 Extrapolator above analyses extracted manuscript text only after your explicit approval. Its proposals and continuity questions need author review. Your Markdown file can be opened in any plain text editor.

Frequently asked questions

What is a series bible?

It records the facts, rules and plot threads that carry across a series. Use it to check continuity while drafting the next book.

Does PubléStudio store my manuscript?

No. Parsing happens in your browser, and extracted text goes to OpenRouter and its selected provider only if you choose to generate.

What files can I use?

Choose TXT, Markdown, DOCX or a text-based PDF. You can also paste a synopsis; scanned PDFs need OCR first.

Which AI model is used?

The default is Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite through OpenRouter. You can choose another model, and your provider sets rates and availability.

Do I need a PubléStudio account?

No. Both modes are public; only the extrapolator requires your own OpenRouter key.
